About the Track

The word Varta originates from Indian languages and denotes news, reporting, or public communication[cite: 4]. News narratives frequently encompass multiple conflicting viewpoints and frame individual stakeholders in highly disparate ways[cite: 5, 6, 9]. Resolving entity-specific sentiment profile tracking is key to structural breakthroughs in fields like computational journalism, media narrative tracking, and subtle media bias observation[cite: 13].

Traditional metrics look blindly at sentence or document levels[cite: 15]. VARTA-2026 offers a specialized benchmark designed to extract highly granular, structured annotations centered around key stakeholders directly out of complex, long-context text environments[cite: 7, 32, 76].

Task Definition

Participants are tasked with evaluating a document and mapping out entity expressions using a structured 5-part architecture framework:

1 Salient Entity: The designated focus target (person, group, or system entity)[cite: 25].
2 What is said ABOUT the entity: The explicit context summaries or implicit opinions targeted toward that entity[cite: 21, 26, 27].
3 What is said BY the entity: Explanatory arguments, reactions, quotes, or viewpoints directly presented by the actor.
4 Polarity of what is said ABOUT the entity: The structured tone class categorization (Positive or Negative).
5 Intensity of what is said ABOUT the entity: The relative emotional magnitude tier (High, Medium, or Low).

Supported Languages

*Additional regional languages may be integrated continuously as validation steps wrap up[cite: 47]. All data tracks conform entirely to open-access FAIR data principles[cite: 59].

Evaluation Metrics

System submissions will be strictly benchmarked across every independent field tier using targeted structural verification[cite: 62]:

  • Entity Extractions: Precision, Recall, and standard F1 Score[cite: 63].
  • Textual Content (About/By): Semantic preservation evaluated using embedding alignment strategies and BERTScore variations[cite: 66].
  • Polarity Assertions: Standard Macro F1 classification tracking across evaluation sets[cite: 64].
  • Intensity Assignments: Categorical accuracy profiles and structural error mappings.
